1

Two console states, one height budget

While a race runs, LIVE owns the column and PREVIOUS compresses to its top three. Between heats there's nothing to watch live — so the start list of the next heat shows compact seeds and PREVIOUS expands into a scrollable results panel: heat places, times, and improvement vs seed. The moment the feed reports a start, it snaps back. Nothing ever overflows the 800pt frame.

2

Every name is a control

Any swimmer name — lanes, On Deck, Previous, the Last Race strip — opens the action popup: race notes, mark DQ, scratch. The name in that popup's header is a second control that opens the profile popup (today's swims, drop, points). Two taps from any race to any swimmer's day. Names underline on press so the affordance is discoverable without decoration.

8

Notes are structured coaching data

The note sheet lists the eight parts of a race a coach actually talks about — start, underwater, breakout, stroke, breathing, turns, pacing, finish. Tap a part, call it good or needs-work, and a write-in line appears for the specifics, labeled either way. What saves isn't a text blob but part + verdict + detail, so a profile can later say “turns flagged needs-work three meets running.”

3

“Yours” is shown, not said

The “— yours” labels are gone. Your swimmers carry the pink 4pt edge, a bold name, and a pink team abbreviation; every other team stays regular weight with its own accent edge. On a bright deck, weight + edge reads faster than a word.

4

The race clock is the display moment

38pt tabular mono. Running lanes tick in Glacial Blue; finished lanes freeze white with their place in Bubblegum. Between heats the clock yields to a READY badge in Glacial — the color itself says “armed, not racing.”

5

Last Race strip: place, time, drop, points

Two snap-scroll cards per view, auto-advancing, pausing on touch. First place reads in Vibrant Pink. The DQ card shows the struck time, the reason, and 0 pts — never color alone.

6

Connection honesty

The footer names the source and latency. If the feed drops it flips amber, running times hide, and estimates are labeled as estimates — per the product's “never silently wrong” rule.

7

Setup & Import keeps the program grammar

Rows and rules, not cards. One pink primary per section. The points table is the app's real 16-place table, editable, with relay 2× and DQ = 0 stated in words.
