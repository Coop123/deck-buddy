Status at a glance

Every event reads FINAL, NOW, or UP NEXT right on the row — no need to open it to know where the meet stands.

Search & filter

Searchable, filterable to your own swimmers — a coach can drop the other five teams from view without losing the full field.

Coach-advanced, too

In manual timing mode, a single “Next heat” tap (undoable) moves Current / Previous / On-Deck instead of waiting on timeline estimates.
