Claim it,then make it yours.

A swimmer links their account to their name on the coach's roster; a parent follows one or more swimmers instead. Either way, a short bio and a SwimCloud id are the only things to customize.

Claim, or follow

Team, then name, then confirm — one screen, three steps. A swimmer claims exactly one roster entry, ever; a parent can tap through the same flow for as many swimmers as they follow.

A parent can edit too

Editing isn't swimmer-only — a parent gets the same bio and SwimCloud-id edit rights for every swimmer they follow, not just a read-only view of them.

Two fields, stored honestly

What claiming actually unlocks

A real claim flow and a real (small) edit surface — plus one genuinely unbuilt piece, named plainly.
