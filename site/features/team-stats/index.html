Three scoring modes, one meet setting

Table 16 (20-17-16…1, standard), Dual (6-4-3-2-1, only the two scoring teams place), and Champs (11-9-8…1, top-10 board) — whichever the meet is set to, standings, results, and this page all follow it automatically.

Relays count once

A relay's points are credited to the relay entry as a single row — never split four ways across the swimmers who swam it — and relay events always score double the individual table.
