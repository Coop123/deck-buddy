On the deck you canseed.

A printed meet sheet is static the moment it comes off the printer. Scratches, relay changes, DQs, results — all of it gets scribbled into the margins by hand, on a clipboard, in the sun, with wet fingers. By the third event the sheet is more edits than original text.

The software built to prevent that problem was built for someone else. Hy-Tek Meet Manager and the rest of that ecosystem run for the meet administrator — at a laptop, indoors, seeding heats and scoring the whole meet. A coach on the deck isn't doing that job. They're tracking a subset of swimmers inside someone else's meet, standing up, moving between events, coaching in the gaps.

Deck Buddy is built for that second job. The meet sheet goes live instead of static — searchable, filtered to your own swimmers, a Now marker sitting over whatever event is running. Everything a coach would otherwise scribble in a margin — scratch, DQ, note, relay swap — happens in place, in seconds, without leaving the sheet. And none of it depends on a signal: offline is the normal case here, not a fallback screen.
