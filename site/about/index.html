About

What we're about.Four rules, kept.

Built for the deck.

Sunlight-readable contrast, 44-point touch targets, and layouts that work with wet hands. If a coach can't read it at noon in lane 4, it doesn't ship.

Offline is the normal case.

Pool decks don't have signal. Everything runs on the device and syncs when a connection comes back — no feature on the deck ever waits on a network.

Honest numbers.

Predictions are labeled ESTIMATE and show their factors. Thin history gets a low-data flag instead of a confident guess. There are zero spinners, and zero invented stats — on the deck or on this site.

Built in the open.

The code, the changelog — with its two-letter dev/design versions — and every decision live on GitHub. Real coach feedback has already removed a feature.

The longer version of why this exists — a paper meet sheet, a wet deck — is told on the story page. The receipts are on GitHub, and we're reachable via contact.
