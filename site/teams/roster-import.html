Teams · Roster import

Paste a CSV.One bad row, zero half-imports.

First name, last name, age, gender — that's the whole contract. It's checked row by row, and it either all lands or none of it does.

The exact contract

Not a rough guideline — this is what the import endpoint actually checks.

firstName, lastName, age, gender

Exactly four columns, comma-separated, in that order. A header row is fine if it's there — the parser recognizes it and skips it; it's also fine to skip it entirely.

Age 4–99, gender M or F

Age has to be a whole number in that range. Gender has to read M or F (case doesn't matter going in — it's stored uppercase). Anything else fails that row.

Atomic, row-numbered rejection

The parser stops at the first bad line and reports it by number. Nothing is written until every row in the paste is valid — a typo on row 12 doesn't cost you rows 1–11.
